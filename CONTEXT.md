# Storyloom glossary

The words Storyloom uses for its own things. Code, tests, and on-screen text use these terms as written.

**Book** — One thing a person makes in Storyloom, saved on their Mac: its pages, words, pictures, and recordings. A book is a *story book* or a *coloring book*.
Avoid: project, document, file

**Story book** — A book of pages with words and pictures, made to be read; the default kind. "Picture book" is the everyday name for what Storyloom makes in general, used in introductions; use "story book" when telling the two kinds apart.

**Coloring book** — A book whose pages are outline drawings to paint in Storyloom or print.

**Page** — One page of a book, with a layout (cover, picture on top, picture on the left, full-page picture, words only, blank) and the elements placed on it.
Avoid: slide, spread

**Element** — One thing placed on a page in the Designer: text, shape, sticker, picture, frame, or sound button.
Avoid: object, layer (a layer is an element's place in the stack, not the element)

**Picture** — An image on a page, imported or drawn by an AI service. "Picture" is the word on screen; `image` is only a field and element-type name in code.
Avoid: image, photo, illustration (on screen)

**Manuscript** — The words of a book written as chapters, before or alongside page design.
Avoid: script, draft

**Chapter** — One section of a manuscript; its words fill one or more pages.

**Character** — A person or creature in a book, with a description and an optional portrait, so writing and pictures keep them the same.

**Character library** — The characters saved across books, to reuse in a new book.
Avoid: cast, avatar library

**Shelf** — A named group of books on the Bookshelf. Deleting a shelf keeps its books.
Avoid: folder, collection

**Page theme** — A set of fonts, colours, and decorations applied to the pages of a book you already have.
Avoid: template (for the theme itself), style, skin

**Starter book** — A ready-made book to begin from, which becomes a new book of your own.
Avoid: template, sample

**Templates** — The screen that offers page themes, starter books, and fonts. Not a thing a book has.

**Story builder** — The screen where you set up a book: title, idea, characters, reading level, and length.

**Designer** — The screen where you lay out each page and its elements.
Avoid: editor, page editor

**Studio** — The screen for narration, background music, and sound buttons.

**Narration** — A recording of a page being read aloud, kept with that page.

**Sound button** — An element that plays a sound when tapped during a read-along.

**Read-along** — Full-screen playback of a book, page by page, with its narration.
Avoid: slideshow, presentation

**Reading level** — Who a story book is written for: first words, early reader, growing reader, or confident reader. It sets word limits.

**AI service** — An online service the person connects to write, draw, or speak for them: their Claude or ChatGPT plan, OpenRouter, fal.ai, or their own. Storyloom has no AI of its own.
Avoid: AI provider, model (a model is what a service runs)

**Budget** — How much an AI service may spend per job: best quality, balanced, or lowest cost. It decides which model is picked.
Avoid: tier (on screen), plan (a plan is the person's ChatGPT or Claude subscription)

**Export** — Saving a copy of a book outside Storyloom: PDF for screens, PDF for printing, e-book (EPUB), or audiobook (WAV).
Avoid: download, publish
